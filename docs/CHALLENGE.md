Business challenge

Nimbus is struggling with slow feature velocity. Growth at a consumer app comes from shipping the right features to the right segments and keeping what wins. The constraint is not ideas but cycle time: spotting that a segment is sliding, deciding which feature to ship, and getting the change live. Every week in that loop is a week of conversion the company does not get back, and a lean team cannot afford to spend it on plumbing. The company moves ~$400M of marketplace throughput a year, so conversion is the lever that matters most, and unoptimized AI and infrastructure spend at scale pressures margin for a team that cannot babysit infrastructure. And the fix has to be AI-assisted without the AI spend running open-ended - at 15M monthly active users, an ungoverned AI feature can become the single largest slice of spend and eat the margin.
